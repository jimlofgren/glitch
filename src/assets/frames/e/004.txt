The ASCII Art generated from 'ASCII Night Sky Generator' is free to use for any purpose, including personal and commercial projects. No attribution is required. However, if you find the tool useful and would like to support us, we'd appreciate a link back or a mention to help spread the word!
Features of ASCII Night Sky Generator
Customizable Moon Phases: Select specific moon phases such as Waxing Crescent, Full Moon, or Waning Crescent, or let the generator randomize them for a natural look.
Asteroid Customization: Choose the type of asteroids to display (Vertical, Small, or Big) and their direction (Left, Right, or Random) to create a personalized night sky.
Big and Small Moon Options: Toggle between small and big moons for added variety and visual impact in your night sky.
Custom Star Probabilities: Adjust the likelihood of different star types, such as dots, plus signs, and asterisks, to control the appearance of your sky.
Width and Height Adjustment: Set the exact dimensions of your ASCII art canvas to create skies from compact to panoramic.
Dynamic Rendering: Regenerate the entire night sky instantly whenever settings are updated, ensuring a seamless user experience.
Fullscreen Mode: Work in fullscreen for a distraction-free creative experience (Premium feature).
Export Options: Download your creation as a .TXT file, or export as an image (.PNG, .JPG, .GIF) with your chosen color theme. Premium users can also export to PDF and SVG.
Retro Color Themes: Preview your night sky in classic styles like Commodore 64, DOS Blue, Terminal Amber, and more.
Custom Object Count: Define the number of moons, stars, and asteroids to include in your night sky. Tailor the density and variety of objects to create anything from a sparse, minimalist scene to a bustling cosmic display.
